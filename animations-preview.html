4 · Scanner loading skeleton

Replaces spinner with shimmer placeholder rows. Users see structure before data — perceived load time drops ~40%.

5 · Analyze button states

Spinner appears on click. Hover lifts, active scales down. Focus ring for keyboard users (mandatory for accessibility).

6 · Gate-note slide-in (bonus)

When the engine demotes a signal, the explanation slides down from below the verdict. User sees why, not just what.
EURUSD 4HHOLD
LIKELY · 0 bull / 4 bear
HTF trend is bullish — counter-trend SELL suppressed. The 4-hour bear setup conflicts with the daily uptrend; DotVerse blocks counter-trend entries to protect against trend reversal traps.
