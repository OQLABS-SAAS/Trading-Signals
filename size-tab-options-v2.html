Real vs Poser — the concept that ties C and D together

"Are the buyers/sellers real, or are they fake?"

Posers move the price superficially without volume. Real money moves the price WITH volume. DotVerse v1 had this on every signal — bringing it back to footprint and order flow.
REAL BUYER
68%
aggressive · ate asks · with vol
POSER BUYER

Verdict: 68% real buyers · 15% real sellers = strong genuine demand. Your BUY signal is backed by real money. If REAL SELLER was the highest %, you'd reconsider.
