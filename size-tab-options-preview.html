A — Market Liquidity

How deep is the market right now?

Tells you whether your ladder of 10 trades will get filled smoothly or slip badly. Pulls live order book depth (Binance for crypto, broker feed for forex/stocks). Without this, you don't know if the market is thin enough that your trades move the price.

B — Contract Size

What does 1 lot actually mean for THIS asset?

Shows the standard contract specs so you know what you're buying. 1 lot of EUR/USD = 100,000 EUR. 1 lot of XAU/USD = 100 oz of gold. 1 lot of S&P 500 e-mini = $50 × index price. Not knowing this = wrong position size.

C — Candle Footprint

Buying vs selling pressure inside each candle

A normal candle just shows open/high/low/close. A footprint shows WHO MOVED THE PRICE — whether the candle was driven by buyers eating asks (green) or sellers eating bids (red). Helps you confirm a signal: a BUY signal on a candle dominated by sellers = trap.
LAST 20 CANDLES · BUY VOL (top, green) vs SELL VOL (bottom, red)
Last 3 candles dominated by buying (tall green, short red) — confirms current BUY signal. If most recent candle was tall RED you'd reconsider.

D — Real-time Order Flow

Live tape of trades going through the market

A streaming feed of every trade as it happens. Shows whether large players ("real money") are buying or selling RIGHT NOW. Most powerful feature, also the heaviest to build (websocket connection per asset).

How to choose

If "I'm a beginner just sizing trades" → just A + B (liquidity + contract size). Tells you the market is healthy and what you're buying.

If "I want to confirm signals before pulling the trigger" → add C (candle footprint). Shows whether the recent candles back up the signal direction.

If "I want to trade like a pro" → all four. D (real-time order flow) is the heaviest build — needs a websocket per asset class — but it's the closest to what prop traders actually watch.
